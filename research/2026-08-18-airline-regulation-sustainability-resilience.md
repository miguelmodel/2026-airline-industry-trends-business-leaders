## 1. Executive Summary

The 2026 operating environment for scheduled passenger airlines is shaped by a set of **enacted** climate and consumer rules that move from announcement to enforcement, alongside **sustainability and resilience conditions** where observed progress is lagging stated targets.

- **Carbon costs step up.** In the EU Emissions Trading System (EU ETS), aviation free allowances are **fully phased out from 1 January 2026**, so covered intra-European flights now purchase every allowance at auction; EU Allowance (EUA) prices traded roughly **€70–95/tonne** across 2025 into mid-2026 [European Parliament press release, 2022](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2022/12/press_release/20221205IPR60611/20221205IPR60611_en.pdf); [euenergy.live, Apr 2026](https://euenergy.live/articles/eu-ets-explained). The global offsetting scheme CORSIA becomes **mandatory in 2027**, ending the voluntary first phase (2024–2026) [EASA CORSIA, accessed 2026-08-18](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia).
- **Fuel mandates are live.** The EU ReFuelEU Aviation regulation requires a **2% sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) blend from 2025 rising to 70% by 2050**; the UK SAF Mandate requires **2% in 2025 rising to 10% by 2030 and 22% by 2040** [EUR-Lex, ReFuelEU summary, updated 2026-04-16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799); [GOV.UK SAF Mandate, updated 2026-07-13](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf-mandate).
- **Supply lags the mandates.** IATA estimates global SAF output near **2.4 million tonnes in 2026 — about 0.8% of aviation fuel use — at ~US$4.3 billion in added cost** [IATA, 6 Jun 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/).
- **Consumer and accessibility rules tighten in the EU and diverge in the US.** The EU adopted a **landmark update to air passenger rights in July 2026** (retaining the 3-hour compensation threshold and adding new transparency and reduced-mobility protections) [Council of the EU, accessed 2026-08-18](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/). In the US, the 2024 automatic-refund rule remains in force, but the 2024 ancillary-fee disclosure rule was **vacated and formally removed by a July 2026 final rule** [Federal Register, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/02/2026-13450/increasing-flexibility-on-disclosure-of-airline-ancillary-fees).
- **Claim integrity is being enforced.** In November 2025, **21 airlines committed to change environmental marketing claims** after a Consumer Protection Cooperation (CPC) Network action coordinated by the European Commission [European Commission, IP/25/2608, 6 Nov 2025](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_25_2608/IP_25_2608_EN.pdf).
- **Resilience pressure is structural.** European air-traffic delays improved in 2025 but remained above target, with **network delay costs exceeding €2 billion** [Eurocontrol, 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/annual-network-operations-report-2025). The US controller workforce stood at **13,164 at end-FY2025, ~6% below 2015** [GAO, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107320). Peer-reviewed research projects **increasing clear-air turbulence** with warming [University of Reading, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2025/Research-News/Turbulent-flights-to-continue-as-warming-world-shakes-skies). Airline **hull-and-liability insurance rates were quoted +15% to +25% for 2026** after heavy 2025 losses [WTW, 7 May 2026](https://www.wtwco.com/en-us/insights/2026/05/insurance-marketplace-realities-2026-spring-update-global-aviation-and-space).

The through-line for 2026 is a **widening gap between binding obligations and delivered capacity** — in SAF supply, carbon-cost absorption, air-traffic staffing, and infrastructure — against long-term demand that ACI projects will roughly double by the mid-2040s [ACI World, 28 Jan 2026](https://aci.aero/2026/01/28/aci-world-releases-global-airport-traffic-forecasts-as-long-term-demand-growth-continues-to-reshape-aviation/).

## 2. Evidence Window and Methodology

**Evidence window:** Sources published or updated primarily in **2025 and 2026**, with selective 2022–2024 legal texts for enacted-rule provenance and selective 2027–2050 milestones for directional implications. All sources were accessed and read on **2026-08-18**.

**Source-selection order:** (1) primary legal texts and official regulator or standards-body pages (EUR-Lex, European Commission, Council of the EU, European Parliament, US Federal Register / Department of Transportation, EASA, ICAO, Eurocontrol, FAA, GAO, ACI World, IATA); (2) audited or association-level industry data (IATA, ACI); (3) peer-reviewed science (Nature portfolio, University of Reading, MDPI); (4) reputable specialist analysis and trackers (WTW, Marsh, Price Forbes, Norton Rose Fulbright, BDO, Beveridge & Diamond, BEUC, Normec Verifavia, carbon-market analysts, regulatory trackers). Community trackers were used only to corroborate or to assemble trajectory tables and are labeled as such.

**Evidence typing:** Each claim is labeled where material as **enacted rule**, **adopted/agreed (pending application date)**, **proposed rule**, **binding target**, **voluntary target/scheme**, **observed outcome**, **estimate**, or **forecast**. Load-bearing numbers were corroborated across at least two sources where feasible; single-source estimates are flagged.

**Validation:** The required WebIQ-MCP gate was completed before drafting — a topic-relevant `web` query returned authoritative EUR-Lex and European Commission results, and `browse` returned meaningful, current content (European Commission ReFuelEU page, crawled 2026-08-18). Additional `browse` deep reads validated the ReFuelEU trajectory (EUR-Lex), CORSIA baseline and cost (EASA), the EU passenger-rights update (Council of the EU), and the greenwashing action (European Commission). `web_fetch` corroborated IATA SAF figures and Eurocontrol summer metrics.

**Exclusions applied:** Political and international-affairs causes, actors, and events were excluded. Several consulted sources (e.g., Eurocontrol annual report, carbon-market notes, insurance broker reports) attributed some effects to such causes; only the neutral operational, capacity, weather, price, loss, or cost evidence was retained.

## 3. Key Findings (Dated)

1. **EU ETS aviation — free allowances end (enacted).** Free allocation to aviation was cut **25% in 2024 and 50% in 2025 and eliminated from 2026**; the aviation cap declines **4.3%/year from 2024 (4.4%/year from 2028)**; **20 million allowances are reserved for SAF uptake through 2030** [European Parliament, 5 Dec 2022](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2022/12/press_release/20221205IPR60611/20221205IPR60611_en.pdf); [Umweltbundesamt factsheet, 2023](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/system/files/medien/11850/publikationen/09_2023_factsheet_aviation_in_the_eu_ets_and_corsia.pdf).
2. **Non-CO2 monitoring is live (enacted).** From **1 January 2025**, the EU ETS obliges aircraft operators to monitor non-CO2 effects (NOx, water vapour, soot, SOx, contrails); the **first non-CO2 report is due 31 March 2026**, with an evaluation in 2027 and a possible legislative proposal in 2028 [Normec Verifavia, 30 Jun 2025](https://normecverifavia.com/news/beyond-co%e2%82%82-preparing-for-aviations-next-eu-ets-challenge/); [European Parliament, 2022](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2022/12/press_release/20221205IPR60611/20221205IPR60611_en.pdf).
3. **CORSIA becomes mandatory in 2027 (voluntary-to-binding transition).** Participation rose from **88 states (2021) to 130 (2026)**; the offsetting baseline is **85% of 2019 CO2 emissions**; the 2024 offsetting obligation equals **15.4% of covered emissions (~55.6 Mt CO2 globally)** [EASA, accessed 2026-08-18](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia).
4. **ReFuelEU Aviation is in operational force (enacted).** SAF blend obligations apply from **2025 (2%)** and rise to **70% by 2050**, with an anti-tankering rule requiring operators to uplift **≥90%** of needed fuel at EU airports [EUR-Lex, updated 2026-04-16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799); [European Commission, accessed 2026-08-18](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-modes/air/environment/refueleu-aviation_en).
5. **UK SAF Mandate is in force (enacted).** In effect from **1 January 2025** at 2%, rising to **10% by 2030 and 22% by 2040** [GOV.UK, updated 2026-07-13](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf-mandate); the Government's consultation outcome sets the **buy-out prices at £4.70/litre (main) and £5.00/litre (power-to-liquid)** and the declining HEFA cap [GOV.UK consultation outcome, updated 25 Apr 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/pathway-to-net-zero-aviation-developing-the-uk-sustainable-aviation-fuel-mandate).
6. **SAF supply lags mandates (observed/estimate).** IATA estimates SAF at **~1.0 Mt (2024), ~1.9 Mt (2025, 0.6%), ~2.4 Mt (2026, 0.8%)**; e-SAF operating/under-construction capacity is **~0.02 Mt against a ~0.6 Mt EU+UK 2030 requirement** [IATA, 6 Jun 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/); [IATA, 9 Dec 2025](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2025-releases/2025-12-09-04/).
7. **EU air passenger rights updated (adopted July 2026; application date pending).** The **3-hour compensation threshold and €250/€400/€600 bands are retained**; new elements include mandatory rerouting timelines, hand-baggage-inclusive fare display, a no-show boarding-denial ban, and stronger reduced-mobility protections [Council of the EU, accessed 2026-08-18](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/); [European Parliament, 7 Jul 2026](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2026/7/press_release/20260703IPR46273/20260703IPR46273_en.pdf).
8. **US consumer-rule divergence (enacted + vacated).** The 2024 automatic-refund rule (14 CFR part 260) remains; the 2024 ancillary-fee disclosure rule was **vacated by the Fifth Circuit and formally removed** by a final rule published **2 July 2026** [Federal Register, 26 Apr 2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/04/26/2024-07177/refunds-and-other-consumer-protections); [Federal Register, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/02/2026-13450/increasing-flexibility-on-disclosure-of-airline-ancillary-fees).
9. **US accessibility rule effective (enacted).** The DOT wheelchair-accommodation final rule (89 FR 102398) took effect **16 January 2025**, strengthening Air Carrier Access Act obligations [DOT, 17 Dec 2024](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2024-12/Final%20Rule%20Ensuring%20Safe%20Accommodations%20for%20Air%20Travelers%20with%20Disabilities%20Using%20Wheelchairs%20-%2089%20FR%20102398.pdf).
10. **Environmental-claim enforcement (observed).** In November 2025, **21 airlines committed** to stop asserting that a flight's CO2 can be neutralised/offset/reduced via consumer contributions or SAF [European Commission, 6 Nov 2025](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_25_2608/IP_25_2608_EN.pdf); [BEUC, 7 Nov 2025](https://www.beuc.eu/sites/default/files/publications/BEUC-PR-2025-034_21_airlines_commit_to_stop_greenwashing_following_a_BEUC_complaint.pdf).
11. **Sustainability reporting narrowed (adopted Feb 2026).** The EU "Omnibus I" raised the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) threshold to **>1,000 employees and >€450m net turnover**, estimated to exclude ~80% of previously in-scope companies [Council of the EU, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/24/council-signs-off-simplification-of-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-requirements-to-boost-eu-competitiveness/); [Norton Rose Fulbright, Dec 2025](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/1679488b/european-parliament-votes-to-adopt-omnibus-proposal-amending-csrd-and-cs3d).
12. **Resilience indicators improved but strained (observed).** European summer 2025 en-route delay per flight fell ~31% year on year yet stayed **above the 0.9 min/flight target (1.88 min/flight Jan–Sep)**, with **~€2 billion** in 2025 network delay costs [Eurocontrol, Summer 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/overview-european-air-traffic-during-summer-2025); [Eurocontrol, 2025 annual](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/annual-network-operations-report-2025). The FAA reset its certified-controller target to **12,563** amid a certified workforce near 11,000 [FAA, 15 May 2026](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-releases-bold-new-air-traffic-controller-hiring-plan).
13. **Climate exposure rising (peer-reviewed projection).** Upper-atmosphere wind shear is projected to **increase 16–27%** and atmospheric stability to fall 10–20% (2015–2100), consistent with rising clear-air turbulence [University of Reading, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2025/Research-News/Turbulent-flights-to-continue-as-warming-world-shakes-skies).
14. **Insurance costs elevated (observed/forecast).** 2025 airline hull-and-liability losses reached their highest calendar-year level excluding 2001, and brokers quoted **+15% to +25% airline hull-and-liability rate movement for 2026** [Price Forbes, Oct 2025](https://www.priceforbes.com/insights-and-event-articles/airline-risk-radar-october-2025-update/); [WTW, 7 May 2026](https://www.wtwco.com/en-us/insights/2026/05/insurance-marketplace-realities-2026-spring-update-global-aviation-and-space).

Sources: [EASA ANCEN background note, Jan 2026](https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/ancen_backgroundnote_1_climateeffectcontrails_jan26_v1.0.pdf); [EASA ANCEN news, 21 Jan 2026](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/newsroom-and-events/news/initial-set-ancen-background-notes-provide-insight-aviations-non-co2); [EASA European Aviation Environmental Report 2025](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/light/topics/european-aviation-environmental-report-2025); [EASA AVENIR WG report, 2025](https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/avenir-wg-pillar-3-final-report.pdf).

## 5. Regulation in Detail

### 5.1 SAF mandates (enacted)

ReFuelEU Aviation is **Regulation (EU) 2023/2405**, applicable since **1 January 2024**, with the fuel-supplier, uplift, airport, and reporting articles (4, 5, 6, 8, 10) applying since **1 January 2025** [EUR-Lex, updated 2026-04-16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799). It obligates fuel suppliers to raise SAF share progressively and requires operators to uplift the fuel needed for the flight to limit tankering.

> "The minimum share of SAF should be 2 % in 2025, rising to 70 % in 2050. The minimum share of synthetic aviation fuels … starts with 0.7 % in 2030, rising to 35 % in 2050."
> - Source: [EUR-Lex — ReFuelEU Aviation summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799)

The Commission's own headline figures state the synthetic sub-target as **1.2% from 2030** (a 2030–2031 average), and note that Switzerland adopted equivalent obligations from **1 January 2026** for Zurich and Geneva [European Commission](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-modes/air/environment/refueleu-aviation_en). The regulation also creates a **voluntary Flight Emissions Label**, verified by EASA under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3170, for display during search and booking [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799).

The UK SAF Mandate operates as a **tradable-certificate scheme** under the Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation, in force since **1 January 2025**.

> "The SAF Mandate starts in 2025 at 2% of total UK jet fuel demand, increasing linearly to 10% in 2030 and then to 22% in 2040."
> - Source: [GOV.UK — Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) Mandate](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf-mandate)

A **buy-out mechanism** caps compliance cost where suppliers cannot secure SAF — the Government's consultation outcome sets that buy-out at **£4.70/litre (main)** and **£5.00/litre (power-to-liquid)** — and a separate **Revenue Certainty Mechanism** (via a SAF Bill progressing through Parliament, with Royal Assent reported as expected around end-2026) is designed to underwrite domestic production [GOV.UK consultation outcome, updated 25 Apr 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/pathway-to-net-zero-aviation-developing-the-uk-sustainable-aviation-fuel-mandate); [GOV.UK essential guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/about-the-saf-mandate/the-saf-mandate-an-essential-guide?LinkSource=PassleApp); [aviationregwatch, May 2026](https://aviationregwatch.com/uk-sustainable-aviation-fuel-act-2026/). The **HEFA cap** (100% of SAF demand in 2025–2026, declining thereafter) reserves room for advanced fuels [GOV.UK consultation outcome](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/pathway-to-net-zero-aviation-developing-the-uk-sustainable-aviation-fuel-mandate).

### 5.2 Carbon markets and carbon-cost exposure (enacted; 2027 transition)

EU ETS free allocation to aviation ended after a staged reduction:

> "The deal includes an end to free allocations of allowances to the aviation sector by 2026 … a decrease of 25% in free allocations is foreseen for 2024 and 50% for 2025."
> - Source: [European Parliament press release, 5 Dec 2022](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2022/12/press_release/20221205IPR60611/20221205IPR60611_en.pdf)

From 2026, covered operators buy all allowances. EUA market prices provide the cost signal: a Reuters survey of analysts placed the **2026 average near €92.65/t (up from ~€75 in 2025)**, while spot prices were **~€72/t in April 2026** within a **€60–95** band — i.e., a wide but clearly elevated cost [CarbonX, Feb 2026](https://carbonx.se/blog/2026-eua-price-outlook); [euenergy.live, Apr 2026](https://euenergy.live/articles/eu-ets-explained). One specialist tracker estimates a **mid-sized carrier's annual EU ETS cost at €70–100 million** once free allowances end — a **single-source estimate** offered for order-of-magnitude only [aviationregwatch, Jun 2026](https://aviationregwatch.com/eu-ets-aviation-free-allowances/).

CORSIA's first phase (2024–2026) is voluntary; the **second phase (2027–2035) is mandatory** for nearly all ICAO states, with exemptions for least-developed countries, small-island and landlocked developing states, and states below 0.5% of global traffic.

> "In 2024, thanks to the change of baseline to 85% of 2019 emissions … the aeroplane operators will be required to offset 15.4% of their CO2 emissions covered by the offsetting requirements. Globally, this represents approximately 55.6 million tonnes of CO2."
> - Source: [EASA — CORSIA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia)

EASA estimates first-phase offset costs at **0.07–0.15% of airline operating costs** and unit prices at **$10–40/tCO2** [EASA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia). Industry commentary anticipates a **~30%+ cost increase into phase 2** and possible eligible-credit undersupply — labeled here as **industry estimate/forecast** [aviationregwatch, Mar 2026](https://aviationregwatch.com/corsia-phase-2-starts-january-2027/).

### 5.3 Consumer, accessibility, and disclosure rules

**EU (adopted July 2026).** The update to rules unchanged since 2004 retains existing compensation and adds transparency and reduced-mobility protections. It followed the Council's **June 2025 negotiating position** and a **June 2026 inter-institutional agreement** that the European Commission welcomed [Council of the EU, 5 Jun 2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/06/05/council-sets-position-on-clearer-and-improved-rules-for-air-passengers/pdf/); [European Commission, 15 Jun 2026](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/commission-welcomes-landmark-agreement-revised-air-passenger-rights-2026-06-15_en).

> "Passengers can be compensated in the event of delay or cancellation. If a flight is more than three hours late or cancelled less than 14 days before departure … €250 … €400 … €600."
> - Source: [Council of the EU — Air passenger rights](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/)

The European Parliament emphasized that negotiators **retained the 3-hour threshold**: "MEPs resisted a push to weaken air passenger rights" [European Parliament, 7 Jul 2026](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2026/7/press_release/20260703IPR46273/20260703IPR46273_en.pdf). New provisions include self-rerouting reimbursement (up to 4× ticket cost if the airline fails to reroute within three hours), assistance thresholds, a **no-show boarding-denial ban**, **free seating for families and companions of reduced-mobility passengers**, and **hand-baggage-inclusive fare display** [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/). Carriers may **reduce compensation by 50% on the longest journeys** when rerouting is offered or arrival delay is under four hours [European Parliament, 15 Jun 2026](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/pdfs/news/expert/2026/6/press_release/20260601IPR44411/20260601IPR44411_en.pdf).

**US (enacted + vacated).** The 2024 combined refund rule strengthened automatic refunds:

> "significantly strengthen protections for consumers seeking refunds of: (1) airline tickets when an airline cancels or significantly changes a flight … (2) checked bag fees when bags are significantly delayed; and (3) ancillary services fees … not provided."
> - Source: [Federal Register — Refunds and Other Consumer Protections, 26 Apr 2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/04/26/2024-07177/refunds-and-other-consumer-protections)

Separately, the 2024 ancillary-fee disclosure rule was struck down and formally removed:

> "This final rule removes all requirements introduced in the 2024 Rule, which are now legally void … This rulemaking is necessary to conform the CFR with the Fifth Circuit's decision."
> - Source: [Federal Register — Increasing Flexibility on Disclosure of Airline Ancillary Fees, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/02/2026-13450/increasing-flexibility-on-disclosure-of-airline-ancillary-fees)

**US accessibility (enacted).** The DOT wheelchair rule took effect **16 January 2025**, adding safe/dignified-assistance definitions and standards for mishandled mobility equipment and transfers under the Air Carrier Access Act [DOT final rule, 17 Dec 2024](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2024-12/Final%20Rule%20Ensuring%20Safe%20Accommodations%20for%20Air%20Travelers%20with%20Disabilities%20Using%20Wheelchairs%20-%2089%20FR%20102398.pdf).

## 6. Sustainability Conditions Beyond Fuel Economics

### 6.1 Targets vs progress (voluntary target vs observed)

The industry's **net-zero-by-2050** commitment relies on SAF for the majority of mitigation, but delivery is behind.

> "global Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) production is expected to reach around 2.4 million tonnes in 2026, representing just 0.8% of aviation fuel use, at a cost to airlines of $4.3 billion."
> - Source: [IATA — SAF Production Volumes Still Disappointing, 6 Jun 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/)

IATA's feedstock assessment concludes that **~500 Mt of SAF is needed in 2050** but that realistic potential is **~400 Mt, a ~100 Mt shortfall**, with technology-rollout pace — not feedstock — the binding constraint [IATA Global Feedstock Assessment, Sep 2025](https://www.iata.org/en/publications/economics/reports/global-feedstock-assessment-for-saf-production/); [IATA, 23 Sep 2025](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2025-releases/2025-09-23-01/). The e-SAF gap is sharpest: EU and UK mandates imply **~0.6 Mt by 2030 against ~0.02 Mt** of operating/under-construction capacity [IATA, 6 Jun 2026](https://www.iata.org/en/pressroom/2026-releases/06-06-saf-production-volumes-still-disappointing/).

### 6.2 Reporting and claim integrity (enacted enforcement + narrowed disclosure)

Environmental-claim enforcement matured in late 2025. Under the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, 21 airlines committed to changes:

> "All these airlines have committed to stop claiming that the CO2 emissions of a specific flight could be neutralised, offset, or directly reduced by consumer financial contributions to climate protection projects or for the use of alternative aviation fuels."
> - Source: [European Commission — IP/25/2608, 6 Nov 2025](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_25_2608/IP_25_2608_EN.pdf)

The action followed a **June 2023 BEUC alert** against 17 airlines; consumer authorities will monitor implementation and may pursue further carriers [BEUC, 7 Nov 2025](https://www.beuc.eu/sites/default/files/publications/BEUC-PR-2025-034_21_airlines_commit_to_stop_greenwashing_following_a_BEUC_complaint.pdf); [Beveridge & Diamond, 18 Nov 2025](https://www.bdlaw.com/publications/eu-enforcement-action-21-airlines-commit-to-reform-environmental-claims-what-aviation-and-non-aviation-businesses-should-know/).

Mandatory sustainability reporting was simultaneously **narrowed** by the EU "Omnibus I" package (final Council sign-off 24 February 2026): CSRD scope rose to **>1,000 employees and >€450m turnover**, reportedly excluding ~80% of previously in-scope firms, while retaining **limited (not reasonable) assurance** [Council of the EU, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/02/24/council-signs-off-simplification-of-sustainability-reporting-and-due-diligence-requirements-to-boost-eu-competitiveness/); [BDO, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.bdo.com/insights/sustainability-and-esg/csrd-post-omnibus-revised-scope-and-requirements); [Norton Rose Fulbright, Dec 2025](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/1679488b/european-parliament-votes-to-adopt-omnibus-proposal-amending-csrd-and-cs3d). Large network-carrier groups typically remain in scope; original "Wave 1" companies continue reporting through the **2026 reporting year (FY2025 data)** unless a member state grants exemption [BDO, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.bdo.com/insights/sustainability-and-esg/csrd-post-omnibus-revised-scope-and-requirements).

### 6.3 Non-CO2 effects (maturing evidence; MRV enacted)

Non-CO2 effects are now inside the regulatory perimeter via EU ETS monitoring from 2025 [Normec Verifavia, 30 Jun 2025](https://normecverifavia.com/news/beyond-co%e2%82%82-preparing-for-aviations-next-eu-ets-challenge/). Scientific bodies emphasize both significance and uncertainty:

> "the contrail cirrus contribution to warming … has a 90% chance of being between 0.01 to 0.05°C, with a best estimate of 0.02°C … Adding together CO2, contrails and other aviation non-CO2 effects gives a contribution of aviation to total warming of about 4%."
> - Source: [EASA ANCEN background note on contrails, Jan 2026](https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/ancen_backgroundnote_1_climateeffectcontrails_jan26_v1.0.pdf)

EASA's expert network notes that most non-CO2 effects are **shorter-lived but can be large in the near term**, and that SAF fuel composition (lower aromatics/sulphur) can reduce soot and particulates [EASA, 21 Jan 2026](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/newsroom-and-events/news/initial-set-ancen-background-notes-provide-insight-aviations-non-co2). A 2026 peer-reviewed analysis examines the "climate opportunities and risks of contrail avoidance," and operational studies note that **blocking flight levels to avoid warming contrails reduces airspace capacity** — linking non-CO2 mitigation to the resilience picture in §7 [Nature Communications, Jan 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68784-8.pdf); [EASA AVENIR WG, 2025](https://www.easa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/dfu/avenir-wg-pillar-3-final-report.pdf).

## 7. Climate, Severe Weather, and Operational Resilience

### 7.1 Turbulence and severe weather (peer-reviewed projection; observed operations)

Multiple peer-reviewed studies project rising clear-air turbulence (CAT) — the leading cause of weather-related in-flight injuries — as jet-stream wind shear intensifies with warming.

> "wind shear will increase by 16-27% and the atmosphere will become 10-20% less stable from 2015 to 2100."
> - Source: [University of Reading, 27 Aug 2025](https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2025/Research-News/Turbulent-flights-to-continue-as-warming-world-shakes-skies)

A 2025 literature review and reanalysis studies corroborate positive CAT trends over key regions, attributed to anthropogenic forcing [MDPI Sustainability, 30 Apr 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/17/9/4065); [npj Climate and Atmospheric Science, 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41612-023-00421-3). On the operational side, European weather-related delays fell in summer 2025 due to **less convective activity**, illustrating year-to-year variability rather than a trend reversal [Eurocontrol Performance Review Report 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/sites/default/files/2026-03/eurocontrol-performance-review-report-2025-es.pdf).

### 7.2 Air-traffic capacity and staffing (observed / plan)

European network performance improved in 2025 but stayed below target:

> "performance remains below the EU target of 0.9 mins of delay per flight, standing at 1.88 mins/flight (1 Jan-15 Sep including summer) … there were still 16 such days [over 200,000 minutes of delay] (Summer 2024: 35)."
> - Source: [Eurocontrol — Overview of European air traffic during Summer 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/overview-european-air-traffic-during-summer-2025)

Eurocontrol attributes residual structural delay chiefly to **air-traffic-controller availability and airspace design**, and lists 2026 priorities of ATCO recruitment, sectorisation, capacity increases, and ATM modernisation; 2025 network delay costs exceeded **€2 billion** [Eurocontrol Flash Briefing, Sep 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/special-flashbriefing-2025); [Eurocontrol annual 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/annual-network-operations-report-2025).

In the US, the FAA reset its certified-controller staffing target and quantified the pipeline:

> "The plan identifies a full staffing target of 12,563 Certified Professional Controllers (CPCs) … As of April 2026, approximately 11,000 CPCs are deployed across more than 300 FAA air traffic facilities, with an additional 4,000 controllers in the training pipeline … It can take more than two years to fully certify a new-hire controller."
> - Source: [FAA — Air Traffic Controller Hiring Plan, 15 May 2026](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-releases-bold-new-air-traffic-controller-hiring-plan)

The GAO found the workforce **~6% smaller than in 2015 while flights grew ~10% to 30.8 million (2015–2024)**, and that only about 2% of applicants ultimately certify [GAO, 17 Dec 2025](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107320).

### 7.3 Airport infrastructure and demand (forecast)

ACI projects sustained long-term demand against mounting capacity constraints:

> "Global passenger traffic forecasted to reach 10.2 billion in 2026 (+3.9% YoY) … projected to double by 2045 and reach 18.8 billion (3.4% CAGR)."
> - Source: [ACI World, 28 Jan 2026](https://aci.aero/2026/01/28/aci-world-releases-global-airport-traffic-forecasts-as-long-term-demand-growth-continues-to-reshape-aviation/)

ACI frames the risk as **infrastructure limitations, aircraft-delivery challenges, operational pressures, and rising sustainability expectations**, with growth increasingly uneven across regions [ACI World Airport Traffic Forecasts 2025–2054 executive summary](https://store.aci.aero/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/WATF-2025-2054-Executive-Summary.pdf).

### 7.4 Insurance and cost exposure (observed / forecast)

Airline hull-and-liability insurance hardened after a heavy 2025 loss year. One broker recorded 2025 as the highest calendar-year airline hull-and-liability claim level excluding 2001, with losses of about **US$1.79 billion through three quarters** [Price Forbes, Oct 2025](https://www.priceforbes.com/insights-and-event-articles/airline-risk-radar-october-2025-update/). For 2026, brokers quoted rate movement of:

> "Airline hull and liability +15% … +25%."
> - Source: [WTW — Insurance Marketplace Realities 2026 Spring Update, 7 May 2026](https://www.wtwco.com/en-us/insights/2026/05/insurance-marketplace-realities-2026-spring-update-global-aviation-and-space)

Brokers cite **claims inflation, rising liability awards, and higher repair costs** (e.g., bird-strike repair costs up ~39% over three years), with US risks facing greater severity; overall market capacity nonetheless remained available for well-managed risks [Marsh, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.marsh.com/en/industries/aviation-space/insights/aviation-insurance-market-pulse-check-june-2026.html); [Insurance Business, 19 Jan 2026](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/aviation-insurance-facing-rising-claims-and-market-shifts--report-562316.aspx). Decarbonization is itself becoming an underwriting factor, with CORSIA introducing **insured-carbon-credit exposures** [Insurance Business, 19 Jan 2026](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/aviation-insurance-facing-rising-claims-and-market-shifts--report-562316.aspx).

## 8. Regional Variation Summary

- **EU / EEA:** The densest regulatory stack — ReFuelEU SAF mandate, EU ETS full auctioning from 2026, non-CO2 MRV, updated passenger rights, environmental-claims enforcement, and CSRD (now narrowed). Switzerland adopted ReFuelEU-equivalent SAF obligations from 2026 [European Commission](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-modes/air/environment/refueleu-aviation_en); [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/).
- **UK:** A distinct SAF mandate paired with a buy-out price ceiling and a Revenue Certainty Mechanism, plus an e-SAF/PtL obligation from 2028 [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-aviation-fuel-saf-mandate).
- **US:** Consumer-protection direction diverges — automatic refunds retained, ancillary-fee disclosure withdrawn — while the accessibility rule is in force and controller-staffing constraints dominate the resilience picture [Federal Register 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/02/2026-13450/increasing-flexibility-on-disclosure-of-airline-ancillary-fees); [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/faa-releases-bold-new-air-traffic-controller-hiring-plan).
- **Global:** CORSIA's 2027 mandatory phase brings ~130 states into offsetting; ACI's fastest growth (and sharpest capacity strain) is concentrated in emerging and developing markets [EASA CORSIA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia); [ACI World](https://aci.aero/2026/01/28/aci-world-releases-global-airport-traffic-forecasts-as-long-term-demand-growth-continues-to-reshape-aviation/).

## 9. Uncertainty and Conflicting Evidence

- **EU e-SAF 2030 figure.** EU sources differ: the EUR-Lex legislative summary states **0.7% in 2030**, whereas the Commission's headline and trackers cite **1.2%** (a reported 2030–2031 averaging effect). The **total SAF endpoints (2% in 2025; 70% in 2050) and the e-SAF 2050 figure (35%) are consistent** across sources [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=legissum%3A4714799); [European Commission](https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-modes/air/environment/refueleu-aviation_en).
- **EU passenger-rights application date.** The update was **adopted in July 2026**, but consulted sources do not state a firm application date beyond publication in the Official Journal; treat provisions as **adopted-but-not-yet-applied** until an application date is confirmed [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/air-passenger-rights/).
- **EUA price dispersion.** 2026 EUA forecasts range widely (spot ~€72 in April 2026 vs analyst-average ~€92.65), reflecting genuine market uncertainty; figures are **market data/forecasts**, not realized full-year averages [euenergy.live](https://euenergy.live/articles/eu-ets-explained); [CarbonX](https://carbonx.se/blog/2026-eua-price-outlook).
- **Per-carrier carbon-cost and CORSIA-cost figures** (e.g., €70–100m/carrier; ~30%+ phase-2 increase) are **single-source industry estimates**; EASA's operating-cost-share range (0.07–0.15%) is the more authoritative anchor [aviationregwatch](https://aviationregwatch.com/eu-ets-aviation-free-allowances/); [EASA](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/environment/icao-carbon-offsetting-and-reduction-scheme-international-aviation-corsia).
- **Delay-metric windows differ.** Eurocontrol's summer figure (3.9 min/flight, Jun–Aug) and its Jan–Sep figure (1.88 min/flight) cover different periods and are not directly comparable [Eurocontrol Flash Briefing](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/special-flashbriefing-2025); [Eurocontrol Summer 2025](https://www.eurocontrol.int/publication/overview-european-air-traffic-during-summer-2025).
- **Court-precedent context.** National court rulings on airline environmental marketing have been reported in EU member states, but no primary court document was independently verified for this report; the EU-wide CPC action (November 2025) is therefore used as the load-bearing enforcement evidence [European Commission, 6 Nov 2025](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_25_2608/IP_25_2608_EN.pdf).

## 11. Research Limitations

- **Adopted-vs-applied timing.** The EU passenger-rights update and CSRD Omnibus are adopted, but exact application/transposition dates were not fully specified in consulted sources; the report labels them accordingly.
- **Forecast dispersion.** EUA prices, 2026 SAF volumes, CORSIA phase-2 costs, and long-run airport traffic are forecasts/estimates with material ranges; single-source estimates are flagged in §9.
- **Metric-window and definitional care.** Delay metrics, SAF "share" denominators (jet fuel vs total aviation fuel), and carbon-cost bases differ by source; windows are stated inline.
- **Excluded attributions.** Some consulted sources (Eurocontrol annual report; several carbon-market and insurance notes) attributed part of the delay, price, or claims environment to political and international-affairs causes. Per scope, those attributions were omitted and only neutral operational, capacity, weather, price, loss, and cost evidence was retained; this necessarily removes some causal context those authors provided.
- **Single-source secondary estimates.** Per-carrier carbon-cost figures (e.g., the €70–100m estimate) and any national court-precedent context rely on secondary sources and are hedged rather than treated as load-bearing.
- **Intermediate mandate steps.** EU ReFuelEU intermediate percentages (6/20/34/42%) and UK 2035 (16%) come from specialist trackers; only the endpoints and the primary-cited anchors are treated as authoritative.
