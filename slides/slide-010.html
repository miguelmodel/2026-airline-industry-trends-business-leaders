Across the evidence, data coordination and quality — not model capability — are the binding constraint on measured value.

The narrative here is a gap between ambition and deployment: intent and spend are at records, but realized, guest-facing value lags, and the 35% and 12.5% deployment figures come from a vendor-sponsored survey of 800 travel and airline professionals — a mixed travel sample — so they should be read as directional rather than precise.

The consistent explanation across independent sources is organizational: fragmented data and weak integration, rather than model limitations, cap the value airlines can measure.

For a technology audience, the insight stays descriptive — the evidence shows AI adoption is deepest inside a single operator's own systems and shallowest where data must flow across partners.

Transition: the commercial results of digital investment are clearest in retailing, loyalty, and payments.
