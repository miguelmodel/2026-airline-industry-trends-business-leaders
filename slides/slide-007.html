Fleet and Operations

The Binding Constraint Moved to Aircraft Supply

A320 family: ~54/mo vs 75 target 737: 42/mo vs 57 target Output ~24% below 2019 ~5,300 fewer than pre-pandemic trend

Reframe the industry's problem: through the mid-2020s the limiter shifted from filling seats to obtaining aircraft, and that constraint is slow-moving.

Clarify the distinction between production and deliveries — the "missing fleet" estimate from IATA and Cirium is corroborated independently by Oliver Wyman's production estimate, and finished airframes are sometimes parked awaiting engines.

The strategic implication for fleet and network planning: capacity discipline is being imposed by supply, which is part of what keeps load factors and asset values high.

Transition: the sharpest piece of the supply constraint is engines and the maintenance needed to keep them flying.
