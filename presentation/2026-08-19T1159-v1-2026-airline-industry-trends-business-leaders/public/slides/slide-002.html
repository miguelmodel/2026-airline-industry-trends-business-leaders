Below the hurdle
A 4.3% return on invested capital sits about 4.2 points under the 8.5% cost of capital.

IATA's December 2025 outlook projected a record $41 billion net profit; by June 2026 that projection was roughly halved to $23 billion at a 2.0% margin.

The two 2026 figures are different forecast vintages published six months apart, not a start-and-end result; the June revision was driven principally by a sharp rise in jet-fuel and other input costs.

Note the paradox for the finance audience: revenue and yields rose while net profit fell, so 2026 is a margin story rather than a demand-collapse story.

Reinforce that even the optimistic December projection sat below the cost of capital, and the June revision widened that gap to a 4.2-point shortfall.

Transition: the demand data under this forecast tells its own two-part story.
