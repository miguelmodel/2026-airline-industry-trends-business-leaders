Position workforce as a decade-long structural theme, not a one-year gap: pilots, technicians, and controllers all face long training pipelines, and the mechanic age profile signals a coming retirement wave.

On reliability, stress that Europe improved but remained constrained by controller staffing, while the US ran a record schedule with slightly higher cancellations; the two systems measure punctuality differently and are not directly comparable.

Keep the two US controller measures distinct: the 13,164 end-FY2025 headcount and its ~6% decline from 2015 are GAO figures, while a separate staffing measure puts controllers roughly 3,000 below target (CBS News); the count and the shortfall use different scopes and should not be merged.

Tie it back to cost: the European delay-cost figure quantifies how staffing and infrastructure limits convert into real operating expense.

Transition: against these physical constraints, the industry is investing heavily in digital capability.
