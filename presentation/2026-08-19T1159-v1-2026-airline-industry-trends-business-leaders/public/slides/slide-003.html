Demand Softened, but Planes Stayed Full

Global RPK — year-on-year change, reported months (2026)
Only Jan, May, and Jun are reported here; intermediate months are not plotted. First year-on-year decline in more than two years.
June load factor — the counterpoint
Capacity stayed constrained, holding utilization near all-time highs.
Weakness was concentrated: excluding one region, June demand fell only 0.6%.

Frame this as observed, realized data rather than forecast; it is the demand backdrop to the profit revision on the prior slide.

Emphasize that most markets remained resilient — international demand excluding the one soft region still rose — so this is dispersion, not a broad downturn.

The record load factor matters strategically: constrained capacity is holding utilization near all-time highs even as headline demand dips, which supports pricing.

Transition: with full planes and rising fares, growth in 2026 became value-led rather than volume-led.
